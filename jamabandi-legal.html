Questions

Questions people ask first

Can someone from outside Himachal buy land?

Restrictions on agricultural land are significant, and a non-agriculturist needs state government permission under Section 118. Rules change, so confirm with a local lawyer or the revenue office before committing.

What is a jamabandi?

It is the record of rights for land in a village. It lists the owner, the khata and khasra numbers, and the area held.

Is a commission added to the price?

The point of this page is to connect buyer and owner directly. Price is agreed between the two of them.

What units is land measured in?

Records in Himachal commonly use bigha, biswa and biswansi, and some places use kanal-marla or square metres. Always compare against the unit written in the record.
